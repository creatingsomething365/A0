Bad Website Analysis

For this assignment, I evaluated a modern website with poor user experience design. You can view the site I chose here: Zara Official Website.

10 Reasons Why This Site is Bad:

  1. Confusing landing page that functions like an image slideshow rather than a clear e-commerce storefront.
  2. Hidden primary navigation that forces users to search for basic product categories.
  3. Poor color contrast where menu text often overlaps directly with background photography, making links unreadable.
  4. Unconventional and erratic page scrolling mechanics that disrupt standard browser navigation.
  5. Lack of clear call to action buttons on product landing pages.
  6. Overly abstract product photography that conceals key garment details from online shoppers.
  7. Nonstandard layout grid that creates a jarring and inconsistent experience across desktop devices.
  8. Slow media load times caused by uncompressed high resolution background video clips.
  9. Small and faint typography sizes that fail accessibility and readability standards.
  10. Poor visual hierarchy that prioritizes editorial art style over straightforward user functionality.
